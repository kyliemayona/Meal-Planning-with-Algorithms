Meal: must be a combination of AT LEAST 3 food items
Food Items are listed in the CSV file

Brute Force Algorithm: Checks every possible combination no matter what. This is very lengthy and can take a long time.

Because of this... there will only be 15 possible food items in the CSV file. ( I do not want my computer to crash )



Ideal Meal: (best case)

cost: minimize
calories: target of 665 
fat: target of 20 grams
carbs: target of 65 grams
protein: maximize
number of food items: at least 3 food items

Reasoning behind the ideal meal:
Cost: the cheaper the better
calories: "2,000 calories a day is used as a general guide for nutrition advice." We used a source mentioned above, along with our own personal knowledge. 2000/3 = 666.6666
Fat: Our team decided on 20 grams of fat
Carbs: Our team decided on 65 grams of carbs
Protein: We have decided that the more protein the better
Number of food items: We dont want the ideal meal to be boring



My idea for this brute force algorithm:

- Need to have a list of food items and their cost, calories, fat, carbs, and protein in the CSV file
- Need to read in from the CSV file
- Generate 2^15 combinations
- Us a loop to count how many food items are included in each combination
- ignore combinations with less than 3 food items
- Add the food item's cost and nutritional information together for EACH combination
- Compare the totals to the ideal Meal
- Create 5 seperate lists containing each meals nutritional information and cost. Each meal will have an identifier that goes with it.
- Sort each list based on its categories goal
    cost: lowest gets 1st place
    fat, carbs, calories: will use absolute value to see which one is closest to my desired amount. Smallest difference between ideal and current will get 1st
    protein: highest gets 1st
- Convert each meal combination's placement in the sorted list into points. 1st place = 1 point
- Add the points from all categories together
- Find the top 8 scores. (The meals with the lowest amount of points are the "best" meals.)


Extra information we have decided: 
- each category is worth the same
- ties will be given the same score. Example: two meals tie for first place, both receive 1 point, and the next meal receives 3 points
